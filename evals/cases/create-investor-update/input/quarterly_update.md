# Quarterly Update Source

Audience: seed investors.
Format: live founder update, 12 minutes.

## Narrative

Linlab shipped the private beta for the agent workspace in Q2. The main message
is that activation is improving, but retention is still early and must be framed
carefully.

## Metrics

| Metric | Q1 | Q2 | Note |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Active pilot teams | 9 | 18 | Two are unpaid design partners |
| Weekly active users | 126 | 284 | Measured over final week of quarter |
| Activation rate | 31% | 44% | User reaches first successful artifact |
| 4-week retention | 18% | 24% | Low sample size |
| Gross burn | $84k | $92k | Includes one-time contractor work |

## Must Include

- Beta shipped on May 14, 2026.
- Team added reusable artifact skills for documents, presentations,
  spreadsheets, and video packaging.
- The biggest product gap is enterprise admin controls.
- Ask: introductions to design partners with high document volume.

## Caveats

- Do not overstate retention.
- Do not claim product-market fit.
- Mention that two pilot teams are unpaid.
