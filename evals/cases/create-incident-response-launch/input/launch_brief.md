# SignalDesk Partner Launch Brief

## Audience and objective

Audience: incident-management consultancies and cloud operations partners.
Objective: explain the product, show the real interface, and recruit five launch
partners for a 60-day enablement cohort.

## Product

SignalDesk gives incident commanders one operating view for service impact,
timeline, responders, and runbook progress. The desktop command center is the
primary product experience. The mobile on-call view lets responders acknowledge
an incident and check the next action away from a laptop.

## Launch facts

- Private beta begins September 22, 2026.
- The launch cohort is capped at five partner firms.
- Each partner may nominate up to three consultants.
- Enablement includes two live workshops and a shared sandbox.
- The cohort is free; no revenue or customer-conversion claim should be made.
- Success is defined as one partner-led incident simulation completed in the
  sandbox within 30 days.

## Required story

1. Open with the SignalDesk product and the September 22 private-beta launch.
2. Show the official desktop command center large enough to inspect its service
   impact map, incident timeline, responders, and runbook progress.
3. Explain the desktop workflow: detect impact, declare the incident, coordinate
   responders, execute the runbook, and communicate recovery.
4. Show the official mobile on-call view without cropping its acknowledgement
   action or recent activity.
5. Explain the five-partner, 60-day cohort and its limits.
6. Close with the ask: nominate one incident-response lead by September 8.
