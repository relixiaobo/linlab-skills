# Draft Product Doc: Team Payout Approval

## Overview

Let teams approve payouts faster.

## Requirements

- Any teammate can approve a payout from the payout list.
- Only finance admins can approve payouts.
- Payout creators can approve their own payout if they are in a hurry.
- Payout creators can never approve their own payout.
- The approval should be fast and secure.
- Store approver_id in the payout_approvals table.

## Prototype

The button says "Submit". If an error happens, handle it gracefully.

## Out Of Scope

Refunds are out of scope.
