# Product Brief Source

Product: Linlab Artifact Skills.

The product helps coding agents produce durable artifacts such as slide decks,
documents, workbooks, PDFs, and videos with domain-specific verification
workflows. The first audience is operators who repeatedly ask agents to produce
client-ready materials.

Key points:

- Users care more about reliable revision loops than manual editing of source
  files.
- Each skill should be independently installable.
- Skills should avoid assuming that other skills are present.
- Verification reports are part of the product value.

Deliverable requested by sales: a short shareable deck and optional PDF handout.
