# Domain: Research Statistics

Use for academic, survey, experimental, social science, psychology, education, and formal statistical analysis.

## First Questions

- What is the research question?
- Is the analysis confirmatory or exploratory?
- What is the unit of analysis?
- Are observations independent, paired, clustered, or repeated?
- What are the dependent, independent, moderator, mediator, and control variables?
- Is there a codebook?

## Method Discipline

- Profile data before analysis.
- Check assumptions before reporting models.
- Use effect sizes and confidence intervals.
- Document exclusions and missing data handling.
- Label exploratory analyses clearly.
- Do not choose the best-looking model without reporting the search.

## Common Outputs

- Descriptive table.
- Assumption checks.
- Model table.
- Figure with uncertainty.
- Manuscript-style paragraph.
- Limitations.

## Pitfalls

- Treating ordinal scales as interval without justification.
- Ignoring survey weights.
- Ignoring nested data.
- Overclaiming causality.
- Reporting only significant results.
- Failing to correct or contextualize multiple testing.
