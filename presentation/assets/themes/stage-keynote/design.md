# Stage Keynote

Use for product reveals, major announcements, company moments, and talks where
one idea must land at a distance.

## Character

- Near-black stage, warm white type, and one high-energy signal color.
- Very large type, severe reduction, and long pauses between evidence-heavy
  frames.
- Media and product evidence appear full-scale rather than inside card grids.

## Signature Moves

- Let one word, number, or object own the frame at key transitions.
- Alternate dark statement pages with inspectable product, proof, or mechanism
  pages instead of keeping every slide sparse.
- Use the accent only for the reveal, change of state, or committed action.

## Adaptation

Derive the recurring stage move from the subject: a product silhouette, signal
change, before/after state, or one expanding system. Do not use this theme for a
reading deck that needs dense evidence on most slides.
