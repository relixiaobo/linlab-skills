# Expressive Neo Grid

Use for founder talks, creative and brand proposals, design-led research,
conference sessions, and stat-heavy stories that should feel contemporary rather
than conventionally corporate.

## Character

- Off-white paper, hard black structure, one electric signal, and one acidic
  secondary field.
- Heavy type, strict grid placement, square geometry, and controlled collisions.
- High density is organized through panels and rules rather than soft card
  chrome.

## Signature Moves

- Let one block break the grid at section turns while all evidence stays aligned.
- Use hard offset fields to emphasize one claim, not every container.
- Treat numbers, labels, and rules as graphic material without sacrificing
  source clarity.

## Adaptation

Choose the signal colors from the subject or brand, then preserve the hard-grid
behavior. Avoid this theme when the audience expects quiet institutional
restraint or when the content is mostly long-form reading.
