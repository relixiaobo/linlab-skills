# Photo Editorial

Use for architecture, places, culture, brand stories, portfolios, field work,
and case studies where real imagery is primary evidence.

## Character

- Full-bleed or edge-led photography with restrained editorial typography.
- Serif display type, precise captions, asymmetric margins, and almost no
  decorative chrome.
- The image carries color; the text field stays quiet and materially connected
  to what the audience is inspecting.

## Signature Moves

- Build section rhythm with full image, diptych, annotated detail, and quiet
  text-led reset pages.
- Let headlines cross or align to an image boundary only when that relationship
  improves reading.
- Treat captions, dates, locations, and provenance as visible composition.

## Adaptation

Define the image program before layout: subjects, aspect ratios, crop policy,
captions, and proof role. If no usable real image exists for a slide, fall back
to an editorial text or evidence layout rather than showing a placeholder.
