# Soft Humanist

Use for teaching, onboarding, healthcare, people and culture, public-interest
work, and consumer subjects that need warmth without becoming childish.

## Character

- Warm paper, humane serif display type, open sans-serif body, and restrained
  botanical or terracotta signals.
- Soft grouping and gentle elevation support comprehension; the page remains
  structured and professional.
- Examples, learner progress, and human consequences matter more than abstract
  corporate symbolism.

## Signature Moves

- Use a recurring learning path, care journey, or capability progression.
- Pair one clear model with a worked example or real human context.
- Use rounded containers only where they clarify grouping; keep page sections
  unframed and avoid card walls.

## Adaptation

Derive warmth from the subject's real people, setting, and language. For formal
board, legal, or transaction work, choose a more authoritative theme unless the
human impact is itself the central argument.
