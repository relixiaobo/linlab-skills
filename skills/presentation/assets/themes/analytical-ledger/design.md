# Analytical Ledger

Use for research, strategy, investment, board, and evidence-heavy material.

## Character

- Quiet authority, high information integrity, and visible source discipline.
- Light paper fields with dark green-black anchors; blue is reserved for a second analytical dimension and orange for risk.
- Strong horizontal rules, explicit table structure, and restrained rectangular labels.

## Signature Moves

- Let one recurring evidence spine connect source, claim, implication, and decision.
- Use a narrow dark risk frame beside a larger factual field on the hardest analytical slides.
- Treat citations and definitions as part of the composition, not as a tiny legal footer.

## Adaptation

Replace the generic evidence spine with a content-specific model: a value chain, depth section, decision funnel, operating loop, or scenario ladder. Do not apply the theme unchanged.
