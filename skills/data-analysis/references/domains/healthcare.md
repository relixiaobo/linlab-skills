# Domain: Healthcare and Clinical Data

Use for clinical research, patient outcomes, biomedical data, trials, epidemiology, and healthcare operations.

## Safety and Privacy

- Treat patient-level data as highly sensitive.
- Avoid exposing direct identifiers and small-cell combinations.
- Suppress or aggregate small groups.
- Document de-identification assumptions.

## Study Design

Clarify:

- Population.
- Exposure/treatment.
- Comparator.
- Outcome.
- Follow-up period.
- Inclusion/exclusion criteria.
- Missingness and censoring.

## Reporting Norms

Use the relevant reporting frame when applicable:

- CONSORT for randomized trials.
- STROBE for observational studies.
- PRISMA for systematic reviews.
- TRIPOD/PROBAST for prediction models.

## Statistical Pitfalls

- Immortal time bias.
- Confounding by indication.
- Informative censoring.
- Competing risks.
- Multiple comparisons.
- Outcome leakage in prediction.
- Site/provider clustering.

## Output

Report cohort flow, baseline characteristics, missingness, effect estimates with CI, sensitivity analyses, and limitations. Do not make medical recommendations beyond the evidence.
