# Domain Path: Grants

## Table Of Contents

- Use When

- Output Shape

## Use When

Use for research funding, grant matching, sponsor discovery, NIH or other funder opportunity research, mechanism selection, funded-overlap analysis, or program officer targeting.

## Intake

Capture:

- research idea and novelty
- field and population/context
- geography or eligible funders
- career stage or organization type
- preliminary data/readiness
- budget and timeline
- known funders or excluded funders

## Source Matrix

Prefer:

- funder official pages and notices
- NIH RePORTER and NIH Guide for NIH work
- Grants.gov for US federal opportunities
- PCORI, DOD CDMRP, NSF, EU Funding & Tenders, UKRI, Wellcome, Gates, or foundation sites when relevant
- funded project databases
- program announcements and eligibility pages
- peer-reviewed literature only for positioning and gap justification

Do not rely on grant listicles when official opportunity pages exist.

## Method

1. Translate the research idea into funder language and keywords.
2. Find active opportunities and recurring mechanisms.
3. Search funded overlap to identify institutes, programs, study sections, and saturation.
4. Match opportunity fit against applicant eligibility and project maturity.
5. Flag deadlines, budget limits, letter-of-intent requirements, and exclusions.

## Output Shape

Include:

- opportunity table
- fit rationale
- eligibility and deadline caveats
- funded-overlap examples
- recommended mechanism/funder order
- program officer or contact recommendation when available
- source audit
