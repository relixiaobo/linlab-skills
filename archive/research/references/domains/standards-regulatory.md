## Use When

Use for regulations, compliance, standards, policy, legal/regulatory status, RFCs, technical standards, government guidance, safety rules, certification, or jurisdiction-specific obligations.

This provides research, not legal advice.

## Intake

Capture:

- jurisdiction
- regulator or standards body if known
- date/currentness requirement
- product/activity being analyzed
- decision context
- whether the user needs plain-language summary or implementation checklist

## Source Matrix

Prefer:

- official law/regulation text and regulator guidance
- official standards bodies: IETF/RFC, W3C, ISO, IEC, NIST, IEEE, ETSI, etc.
- government databases and gazettes
- regulator enforcement actions and official FAQs
- court decisions where relevant and jurisdiction-specific
- reputable legal/technical commentary only as interpretation, not primary authority

## Method

1. Lock jurisdiction and date.
2. Find controlling primary text.
3. Find official guidance or implementation materials.
4. Separate binding requirements from guidance and commentary.
5. Track effective dates, transition periods, exemptions, and scope.
6. Flag when professional legal/compliance review is needed.

## Output Shape

Include:

- jurisdiction and date basis
- controlling sources
- requirements versus guidance
- applicability analysis
- open issues and caveats
- implementation checklist when useful
