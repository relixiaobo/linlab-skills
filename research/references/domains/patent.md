## Use When

Use for prior art, patent landscape, novelty search, freedom to operate, patent portfolio diligence, litigation prior-art hunting, claims comparison, or patent assignee analysis.

This produces research signal, not legal advice. Recommend qualified patent counsel for filing, licensing, or litigation decisions.

## Intake

Capture:

- invention or technology description
- purpose: novelty, FTO, competitive landscape, diligence, litigation prior art
- jurisdictions
- known prior art or target patent numbers
- priority dates if known
- risk tolerance

## Source Matrix

Prefer:

1. Google Patents
2. Espacenet
3. USPTO Patent Public Search and assignment records
4. WIPO Patentscope when international/PCT matters
5. Lens.org for citation graph and family data when available
6. original papers and technical docs for non-patent literature

## Method

Search strategy depends on purpose:

- novelty: narrow claim-text and concept queries plus synonyms
- FTO: active patents, jurisdictions, claim text, expiration/abandonment status
- landscape: broad technology queries, CPC classes, assignee trends
- diligence: assignee, subsidiaries, inventors, assignment chain, families
- litigation prior art: target claims, priority date, same CPC and adjacent language before priority

Extract independent claims for closest art. Group patent families to avoid double-counting.

## Output Shape

Include:

- purpose and legal caveat
- search strategy
- closest prior art table
- claim overlap or differentiation
- jurisdiction and status notes
- assignee/family/citation signals
- risk flags
- audit of searched databases
