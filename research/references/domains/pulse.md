## Use When

Use for current conversation, sentiment, community reaction, trend discovery, platform-specific discussion, pain points, buzz, controversy, or "what are people saying about X?"

Pulse captures conversation signal. It is not canonical truth.

## Source Matrix

Choose platforms based on the topic:

- Reddit for user sentiment, complaints, workflows, consumer or practitioner pain
- Hacker News for technical critique, developer sentiment, startup/AI/tool discussion
- X/Twitter for fast-moving public conversation when accessible
- GitHub issues/discussions for developer tools and open-source projects
- Discord/Slack/forum communities only when accessible and appropriate
- App stores, Product Hunt, G2/Capterra, Steam, Trustpilot for product sentiment
- news/trade press for broader context
- official sources only to verify factual claims discovered in conversation

## Method

1. Define topic, angle, time window, and platform scope.
2. Search platform-specific queries.
3. Record engagement and recency where available.
4. Cluster repeated themes.
5. Separate consensus, controversy, pain points, excitement, and misinformation.
6. Verify factual claims against stronger sources before repeating them as facts.

Cross-platform agreement is stronger than a single viral post. High engagement is not the same as truth.

## Output Shape

Include:

- time window and platforms searched
- highest-confidence conversation signals
- recurring pain points
- excitement or adoption signals
- controversies and minority views
- factual claims that were verified separately
- caveats about representativeness
