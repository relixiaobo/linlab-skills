## Use When

Use for decision-grade research on a company, person, nonprofit, government organization, project, or institution: diligence, meeting prep, red flags, reputation, background, hypothesis testing, investment/acquisition/sales/partnership context.

Do not use for generic "what is this company?" unless the user wants a decision-grade dossier.

## Intake

Capture:

- exact subject identity and disambiguator
- subject type: person, company, nonprofit, government org, project, other
- purpose: sales, investment, acquisition, hiring, journalism, competitive intelligence, other
- hypothesis to test, if any
- time horizon and sensitivity exclusions

If the subject is ambiguous, stop and ask for a disambiguator.

## Source Matrix

Company:

- official website, product pages, blog, docs
- investor relations, annual/quarterly reports, SEC EDGAR or local filings
- press releases and newsroom
- court/regulatory records when relevant
- GitHub and package registries for technical organizations
- hiring pages and LinkedIn for operating signals
- credible news and trade press
- Crunchbase/PitchBook/SimilarWeb or similar databases when available, labeled as aggregator data

Person:

- official bio, employer page, personal site
- LinkedIn, GitHub, Google Scholar, ORCID when relevant
- talks, podcasts, interviews, publications
- company filings or court records only when relevant and ethical
- news/trade press

Nonprofit:

- official site
- Form 990 or equivalent registry
- ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer or local charity regulator
- grant databases
- news/trade press

Government org:

- official government site
- budgets, audits, procurement databases
- regulator reports
- news/trade press

## Method

Use hypothesis-driven research. For material decisions, allocate at least 30% of effort to disconfirming evidence.

Classify each key source as primary, secondary, or tertiary. Do not treat marketing claims, logo walls, or unsourced database entries as confirmed facts.

Build a timeline for the last 12-24 months when recency matters.

## Output Shape

Include:

- identity and scope
- hypothesis verdict: supported, partial, disproven, or inconclusive
- evidence balance: supporting, disconfirming, unresolved
- timeline
- operating signals
- red flags and caveats
- conversation hooks or decision implications
- source tier audit
